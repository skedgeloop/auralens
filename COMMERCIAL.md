## Why a Commercial License Exists

AuraLens is dual-licensed. The open-source version (AGPLv3) requires that any product
built with AuraLens — including web apps, SaaS platforms, and hosted services — must
also be fully open-sourced under AGPLv3.

Most commercial products cannot meet this requirement. A Commercial License removes
the AGPLv3 obligation entirely, giving you full rights to embed, modify, and deploy
AuraLens inside your closed-source product without disclosing your proprietary code.
